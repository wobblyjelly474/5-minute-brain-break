# 5-Minute Brain Break 🧠

An interactive, self-paced **5-minute cognitive distraction task** developed as part of a **4th Year Biomedical Engineering Final Year Project (FYP)** at **Sri Sivasubramaniya Nadar College of Engineering (SSN College of Engineering)**.

## Purpose

This website is designed to provide research participants with a short, engaging activity between experimental tasks. It presents general knowledge, science, human health, history, geography, and popular-culture questions as a brief cognitive distraction activity.

## Features

* 🧠 30 multiple-choice questions
* ⚡ Instant feedback after each response
* 📊 Progress indicator
* 🎯 Self-paced interaction
* 📱 Suitable for desktop and mobile use
* 🔒 No login or personal information required
* 🌐 Runs entirely in the browser

## Research Use

The website is intended to serve as a standardized **5-minute cognitive distraction activity** within the FYP research protocol.

The activity is designed primarily for participant engagement and distraction rather than assessment of general knowledge. Immediate feedback is provided after each response to maintain engagement throughout the task.
